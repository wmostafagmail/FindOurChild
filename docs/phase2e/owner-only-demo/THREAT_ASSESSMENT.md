# Owner-only demo: threat assessment and residual risk

**Design assessment, not an independent security assessment, completed penetration test or approval to execute.** Read the [proposed policy](POLICY_AMENDMENT.md) and [contract](AUTHORIZATION_CONTRACT.md). All V3 enforcement and tests remain pending. Original findings and evidence remain intact.

Protected assets are real approval identity, immutable scope/fixture history, artifact integrity and permission, private demo media/vectors/results, tenant/case boundaries and trustworthy reports. Adversaries include an external attacker, cross-tenant/reporter user, compromised owner session, mistaken or malicious operator, forged measurement producer, upstream supplier, stale worker and privileged host/store administrator.

## Changed trust assumptions

V3 intentionally gives one human both operator and approval authority. It removes the second person's opportunity to detect dishonest evidence, poor technical judgment, unsuitable fictional labels and cherry-picking. Owner MFA protects the identity boundary; it does not establish expertise, genuine inspection, independent review or scientific blinding. No automation can prove what the owner learned from external model scores or whether a publisher's provenance statement is true.

Technical source/byte auditors must remain separate measurement implementations/processes where specified, with reproducible evidence; this is **measurement separation**, not a second human or a claim of organizational independence. A compromised host or measurement tool can falsify receipts unless independently protected source/build/custody checks detect it. Hashes and MFA do not eliminate that risk.

## Required risk acknowledgments

The next owner decision must expressly accept or revise the loss of independent human review, the owner/IdP/host concentration of trust, uncertain fictional identity/provenance and inability to prove external score blindness. Those acknowledgments cannot waive byte validation, isolation, actual native failures, mandatory tests, case authorization or prohibited actions.

Operational/research and existing NGO safeguarding approval separation is unchanged. A real owner may approve demo execution and still be denied a case, image or sensitive conclusion. This policy never authorizes custody, release, automatic identity decisions or real-child processing.

The original human A1b review remains unmet; author automation is not an independent assessment. All real assets, actual native loading/inference, V3 deployment, operational approval and Phase 2F/3 remain outside this design checkpoint.
