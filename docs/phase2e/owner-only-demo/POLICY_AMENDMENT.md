# Proposed amendment: one owner authority for the isolated demonstration

**Status: design only; not approved for implementation or use.** This is a new exception alongside the [accepted V2 proposal](../DEMO_APPROVAL_POLICY_PROPOSAL.md), [V2 implementation](../DEMO_GOVERNANCE_IMPLEMENTATION.md) and [original A0–A8 gates](../ARTIFACT_ACQUISITION_GATES.md). Those records and executable validators are preserved without edits.

## Context, options and consequences

The owner has accepted the governance engineering milestone but now wants an isolated working demonstration without appointing a second human or operating a multi-person signing ceremony. V2 cannot meet this goal by changing a role flag: its Ed25519 chain, enrollment and score-access transitions deliberately enforce O/T/F separation. Treating an owner as fictional T/F identities would falsify review evidence.

Considered options: retain V2 unchanged (retains a second-person dependency); remove its conflict checks (would erase a reviewed boundary and invite cross-policy replay); or implement a separate owner-authenticated policy with the same mandatory technical evidence. **Propose the separate policy.** It reduces approval overhead and loses independent human error detection. Automated measurements and owner consent do not replace independence, native-security expertise or scientific validation.

## Exact proposed clauses

1. **Scope.** This amendment applies only to the isolated fictional-data Find Our Child demonstration, the exact three dlib component references and 48 image references in the frozen [asset proposal](../../planning/demo/DEMO_ASSET_PROPOSAL.json). The 68-point model, alternative weights, arbitrary images/URLs, blocked model profiles, operational/research environments and their credentials remain excluded. Referenced permissions must be reverified before any later acquisition; historical publisher statements are not a fresh clearance.
2. **Authority.** The actual project owner, identified by the existing pinned issuer/audience/subject and fresh MFA boundary, is the sole human demo approval authority. The owner may also operate the tools. No T/F appointment, second person, reviewer/operator inequality or human Ed25519 enrollment is required **by V3**. No assistant, service identity, extra account or fabricated reviewer may impersonate the owner.
3. **One explicit authorization.** Replace the human V2 policy/scope/A1b-demo/A2–A7 permit chain with one immutable, narrowly scoped owner authorization per bounded campaign lease. It states exact deployment, source/build, artifact plans, frozen fixture plan, action subset, resource/processing limits, retention, uncertainty and expiry. The existing owner/MFA boundary authenticates a deliberate confirmation of that exact digest. Automated stage receipts supply evidence, not additional human approvals. Expiry or scope changes require a new explicit owner action; there is no perpetual or automatic renewal.
4. **Mandatory technical conjunction.** Owner authority is necessary but insufficient. Source/license disposition, byte identities, safe unpacking, real malware scanning, sandboxed decoding, immutable provisioning, native parser qualification, hard isolation, current case/purpose grants and mandatory audit must all pass at their respective boundaries. No owner waiver, `passed=true` field, successful HTTP response or provider switch can override a failed/missing technical requirement.
5. **Pre-run fixture selection.** The single owner authorization binds a complete fixed selection: 48 source-image identities, 24 fictional pairings/groups, independent per-image provenance and uncertain dates/ages, gallery/query membership, splits, duplicate policy, failure handling and evaluation/preprocessing plan. These are explicit owner-declared demonstration labels, not independently established identities. Source evidence must support synthetic provenance; unknown/contradictory media origin is held. No inference is allowed merely because a caller labels an image synthetic.
6. **No score-informed replacement.** Selection and pairings become immutable when authorized and must be sealed again against actual clean-byte lineage before **any** selected model processes an image, including detection-only diagnostics. An atomic first-image-run tombstone is persisted before native admission. No image, link, split, gallery, preprocessing/evaluation choice or ranking-relevant metadata can then be replaced to improve results. A new UUID, owner session, authorization, restored backup, model-space alias or expiry renewal cannot reset that history. All selected failures, no-face/multiple-face outcomes, withdrawals and missing results remain in the ledger and denominators. If a selected item fails a mandatory gate, hold/abort; never silently substitute or reduce the set.
7. **Lifecycle and custody.** Preserve authenticated owner binding, current session/account status, authorization epochs, expiry, explicit revocation, deployment/tenant scope, append-only audit, external checkpoint reconciliation, inactive restore and fail-closed reads/publication. Private workers remain offline and receive bounded capabilities, not owner bearer tokens, approval-writing privileges or operational DB credentials. Missing authority, audit, trusted time or isolation stops dependent work.
8. **Unchanged application safeguards.** V3 approval grants no case assignment, media access or investigative role. Existing NGO/tenant/case/purpose/MFA checks, human review histories and independent safeguarding stages remain required. Sensitive conclusions, automatic identity confirmation, public biometric search, custody disclosure, child release, real-child data and operational activation remain disabled. An owner without a current case/media grant is denied like any other caller.
9. **Honest exception and evidence labels.** The original A1b independent human review remains unmet with its demo waiver. V3 additionally replaces independent A3/A4/A5 technical approval and blinded A6 second-person approval with actual technical measurements and an owner-frozen fixture declaration. No completed independent review, reviewer competence, human signature, actual model hash, scan/load result or child-identification accuracy is inferred. The owner may inspect clean images and record a safety hold or uncertainty note; that is not a second permit or permission to change the selection. Prior external score knowledge cannot be technologically disproved and must not be represented as scientific blinding.
10. **Performance and phase limits.** Retain the original database p95 target of 1,000 ms and accepted demo exception. The accepted main sample of 680.841914 ms does not resolve historical misses (1,149.158629/1,126.882086 ms; implementation-head push 1,102.589825 ms). No native CPU/memory/latency, security or correctness exception follows. Full Phase 2E, its later model workflows, Phase 2F and Phase 3 remain unaccepted/unstarted by this packet.

## Selection corrections and renewal

Before any image-model call, a legitimate non-score correction requires cancelling the immutable authorization and creating a new explicitly reviewed scope; the rejected version and reason remain. After the first call, the campaign's selection and label identity cannot be rewritten. Append-only safety/uncertainty errata can explain a problem and withdraw results without altering the frozen inputs or improving a reported denominator. A genuinely new dataset or changed evaluation protocol needs a separately reviewed future plan; this amendment supplies no generic reset path.

An identical-plan renewal binds the authentic original fixture/first-run ledger and current epochs. Renewals do not claim a new blind review and cannot reset rejection, prior score exposure or revocation. License/source revocation always wins over continued availability: cancel work, deny projections and follow existing cleanup/retention controls.

The freeze lookup is project-wide and keyed by stable approved source-image lineage. Pairings, partitions, model/space names, authorization/run IDs, deployment instances and epochs must not create a new lookup key. They are checked against the original sealed value. A new deployment or a relabeled plan therefore cannot evade the first-run history.
