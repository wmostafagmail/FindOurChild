# Child safeguarding policy — proposed

Requires approval by the NGO safeguarding lead, child-protection authority liaison and qualified Egyptian counsel before operational use. This is a design specification, not an approved operating policy.

## Governing rules

The child's welfare takes precedence over a claimant's desire for information. Reports, family assertions and AI leads remain unverified until qualified evidence review. No public biometric search, shelter directory, current-location lookup, live face surveillance or automatic custody decision is permitted.

Contact verification proves control of a channel, not guardianship. A genuine family relationship does not establish that return is lawful or safe. Children may face abuse, coercion, trafficking or disputed custody, including risks from an actual guardian. Trained staff listen to the child's account and preferences through an age-appropriate, trauma-informed process without promising reunion.

## Intake and evidence

Accept incomplete reports without pressuring a child or finder to invent names/dates or obtain unsafe photographs. Photos follow approved safeguarding procedures and avoid exposing other children. Individual finders receive a safe reporting route and NGO relay; the system never advises informal custody transfer or private meetings.

Intake staff assess urgent safety, suspected abuse, coercion and claimant conflicts before ordinary matching. Emergency referrals use locally approved, maintained official guidance. Abuse allegations route to a restricted safeguarding queue separate from the implicated claimant or staff member.

Encrypt originals, retain provenance and show sanitized review copies. Record statements as attributed claims; independently verify supporting authority/police references. Unknown or conflicting evidence stays visible. Multiple-face selection requires staff confirmation; model output cannot establish which child a photograph depicts.

## Human decision gates

| Gate | Required record | Responsible decision-maker |
|---|---|---|
| Source/contact verification | Verified channel, reporter relationship claim and evidence provenance | Trained intake reviewer |
| Identity investigation | Independent documentary/context evidence, reviewed contradictions and AI limitations | Qualified caseworker and independent reviewer |
| Claimant risk assessment | Relationship/guardianship evidence, appropriate child interview, custody dispute and fraud assessment | Safeguarding lead and competent authority |
| Disclosure | Named recipient, exact fields, purpose, expiry, evidence snapshot and bilateral approvals for federation | Independent safeguarding approver(s) |
| Custody authorization | Current competent-authority documentation and independent verification | Authorized authority liaison under legal protocol |
| Reunion plan | Safe supervised handover, transport, witnesses, child support and fallback arrangements | Safeguarding approver with authority approval |
| Completion | Witnessed handover evidence and welfare follow-up plan | Authorized staff independent of claimant |

A requester cannot approve their own disclosure or reunion plan. A principal holding two roles still counts as one person. Approvals bind claimant, child/case, action, field scope, evidence/plan revision and expiry; changes invalidate approval. A general admin override cannot bypass these gates. Break-glass welfare access is scoped and audited and cannot authorize custody transfer.

The platform records decisions made by competent humans; it confers no legal authority. Identity confirmation, limited contact, disclosure and custody transfer are separate outcomes. Evidence supplied from an external authority requires verification even if that authority does not use the platform.

## Fraud, misconduct and appeals

Repeated claimant attempts, contradictory evidence and possible document tampering are human-review flags, not automatic accusations. Unverified claimants cannot learn whether a found child exists. Reporter ownership of a missing report never authorizes shelter/custody disclosure.

Conflicts of interest require reassignment. Provide confidential abuse reporting and independent review/appeal without disclosing the child's location. Pause suspicious disclosure workflows while maintaining care and documenting authority escalation. A harmful or disputed family relationship can justify withholding reunion despite confirmed identity.

## Training and oversight

Sensitive staff need safeguarding training, named MFA accounts, purpose-based grants and regular access reviews. Proposed monthly pilot reviews examine false leads, improper disclosure, rejected claims, child complaints and workload. Serious harm signals permit pausing matching and contact workflows.

Publishing an appeal, child photo or reunion story is a separate legal/safeguarding decision outside this initial scope. Production-photo training and synthetic age progression require separate approval. This engineering policy cannot substitute for an NGO's legally reviewed operating procedure.
