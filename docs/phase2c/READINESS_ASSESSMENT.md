# Phase 2C qualification-readiness assessment

Assessment date: 2026-10-27. Scope: documentary assessment after Phase 2B engineering, not implementation of Phase 2C or permission to acquire or execute a model. The exact-main acceptance evidence is recorded separately in [Phase 2B acceptance](../phase2b/ACCEPTANCE.md) and [its independent verification](../phase2b/main-merge-verification.json).

## Decision

**Ready for further synthetic engineering and approval preparation; not ready for real-model qualification or child-data evaluation. Operational recognition remains disabled.** All eight model/component profiles remain blocked; no real recognizer has been downloaded, executed, qualified or benchmarked. No approved longitudinal child dataset, signed evaluation authorization, or independently validated biometric operating threshold is established by the repository evidence reviewed. This assessment approves no acquisition request and selects no operational candidate. An absence of documentary evidence is an unresolved gate, not a finding that a particular activity is lawful or unlawful in every jurisdiction.

Phase 2B engineering acceptance covers implemented synthetic controls and their regression evidence. It does not clear model rights, training data, ethics, dataset access, scientific validity or operational use. No production model, public biometric search, automatic identity confirmation or custody disclosure is authorized. This change adds documentation only: no application, API, model registry, migration, dependency, worker, UI or feature flag changes.

## Remaining biometric validation

Before any qualification decision, independently measure Recall@1/10/50 on mate-present private-gallery queries; FPIR on genuinely absent open-set subjects; FNIR at frozen calibration-only thresholds and K; and failure-to-acquire/enroll/detect/align, quality rejection and timeout counts in intention-to-evaluate denominators. Similarity is not an identity probability. Measure both missing-to-found and found-to-missing research directions; neither becomes an operational endpoint.

Cover infancy through adolescence and both age-gap directions using actual separately authorized capture events, including unknown/cross-bin intervals. Preserve image-level metadata rather than one age per child. Measure pose, occlusion, resolution/quality, authorized demographic strata/intersections, unknowns, subgroup limitations and source bias. Do not infer protected attributes from faces or treat empty/underpowered strata as covered.

Freeze multi-photograph budgets and aggregation before scores; calibrate gallery-size/photo-budget arms separately. Report false leads and NGO review workload at K=1/10/50, incorrect-match rejection and human disagreement without confirming identity. Subject/family-cluster uncertainty, rare-event power and paired comparisons need an independent statistician; repeated pairs do not create independent children. Current synthetic score checks and upstream adult/general benchmark claims establish no longitudinal-child accuracy.

Require artifact/component/provider/precision and embedding-space identities, exact-search baseline before ANN loss measurement, sealed label/split/protocol hashes, deterministic selection/ties, seeds, failure/exclusion accounting and independent rerun evidence. No approved real threshold, detector quality cutoff, GPU capacity or demographic robustness result exists yet. A future approved evaluation can still conclude that no candidate qualifies.
