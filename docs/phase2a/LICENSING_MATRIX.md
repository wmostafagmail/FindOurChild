## Candidate reassessment

InsightFace/ArcFace remains a technically useful comparison candidate after clearance. Reviewed orchestration/provider/preprocessing source does not clear the buffalo model pack or its detector. ArcFace names a family of methods; it does not identify a weight artifact or license. No server/person-tracking/liveness products are adopted. NGO/nonprofit status is not treated as permission for the restricted weights.

AdaFace remains a useful low-quality baseline candidate, with BGR normalization and checkpoint-loading risks explicitly separated from its MIT source license. The reviewed inference loads the IR50 MS1MV2 checkpoint through `torch.load`; that operation was never executed. Exact checkpoint terms, alignment component provenance, training sources and a safely pinned modern inference runtime remain prerequisites. Quality-adaptive training is not evidence of age invariance.

DeepFace is a multi-backend wrapper, not a uniquely licensed trained model. Its current pinned tree moved ArcFace/SFace implementations into backend-specific ONNX modules, unlike the Phase 0 source layout. The initially requested old paths were unavailable; the official tree resolved the new paths and all 22 final selected text sources were retrieved and hashed. Reviewed wrappers can trigger external weight downloads. Neither the wrapper nor any backend was imported or installed. Detector, recognizer, preprocessing, dependencies and artifacts require independent qualification.

OpenCV SFace is an additional lightweight candidate: the selected directory expressly declares Apache-2.0 for its files, and the companion YuNet detector directory carries MIT. The exact prospective float ONNX artifact is distinct from INT8/block-quantized variants, each requiring its own checksum, manifest and parity evaluation. Its README does not supply the complete exact training-data grant/provenance needed here. No threshold or upstream accuracy table is adopted as a child-safety result.

AuraFace-v1 is another ArcFace-derived candidate. The pinned publisher repository declares Apache-2.0 and describes commercially sourced training; exact dataset identities, subject authority and full rights chain are not supplied in the reviewed card. The repository also contains detector/landmark/attribute artifacts: a recognition weight declaration is not blanket clearance for every bundled component. No automatic snapshot download or attribute inference is used.

CompreFace, Immich and PhotoPrism remain architecture references under Phase 0 decisions; a service wrapper cannot remove underlying recognition-weight/data restrictions. Qdrant and alternate orchestration remain deferred until measured need. No candidate is selected as operationally qualified.

## Required artifact release record before any future model use

Require exact publisher/repository revision, weight filename and immutable binary hash, code/component notices, weights grant covering the proposed purpose, detector/aligner terms, named training datasets and their exact terms/provenance, permitted subjects/purposes/retention, independent legal/data-protection approval, safe serialization/runtime/provider/container pins, offline verification, numerical/preprocessing parity and a separately approved evaluation protocol. Unknown or conflicting rights fail closed. Do not contact publishers or transmit data without separate authorization.

Model benchmark status for all five real candidates: **not run**. Weight hashes and exact training-data entitlements remain missing evidence, not inferred approvals. Real accuracy, fairness, age-gap generalization and operational thresholds are unmeasured. The weight-free synthetic adapter has no training dataset and serves only engineering validation.
