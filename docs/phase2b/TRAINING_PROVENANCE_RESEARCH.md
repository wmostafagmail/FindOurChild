# Additional training-provenance investigation

Observed 2026-10-26; human documentary evidence only, not runtime clearance. [Versioned observations](provenance-supplement.json) retain exact author-repository revisions, text hashes and URLs. No weights, datasets, code execution or image downloads were involved.

The [original SFace author's README at `cb5ffb3c4863eecb3abf6a80529daffb05a0e820`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zhongyy/SFace/cb5ffb3c4863eecb3abf6a80529daffb05a0e820/README.md) names CASIA-WebFace, VGGFace2 and MS-Celeb-1M as experimental training datasets. That establishes method-level experimental lineage; it does not identify the complete exact training corpus or permissions for OpenCV's separately exported `face_recognition_sface_2021dec.onnx`. Missing GitHub license detection is not proof that no license exists, and OpenCV's directory declaration is not a substitute for the corpus/redistribution chain. Exact artifact-to-training-log mapping and subject/data rights remain unresolved.

The [AdaFace author's related CVLFace IR50/MS1MV2 card at `aa35c74e86afb7482cb7859af4c14eab83a8728d`](https://huggingface.co/minchul/cvlface_adaface_ir50_ms1mv2/raw/aa35c74e86afb7482cb7859af4c14eab83a8728d/README.md) explicitly directs users to the training dataset's license. Its safetensors/custom-code release is a possible future serialization investigation, not a cleared replacement for the original Drive checkpoint. Require a distinct artifact manifest, identity/conversion equivalence evidence and all dataset rights. Do not run `AutoModel`, remote custom code, `trust_remote_code` or automatic hub downloads to test it under this phase.

The [original Oxford VGGFace2 page](https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~vgg/data/vgg_face2/) describes web-image-search acquisition and says its download links are no longer available. This does not authorize replacement downloads from third-party mirrors or establish subject authority. The [Oxford VGG-Face descriptor page](https://robots.ox.ac.uk/~vgg/software/vgg_face/) describes non-commercial research restrictions for its own models; do not transfer that statement to a differently trained FaceNet512/ONNX asset by name similarity. The [original VGG-Face dataset page](https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~vgg/data/vgg_face/) also notes source-population bias and unavailable download links. These are supporting provenance leads, not proof that every reviewed candidate uses that exact dataset/version.

InsightFace's pinned model-zoo source names WebFace600K for the prospective buffalo_l recognition artifact; exact dataset edition, original source agreements, removals and authority remain unresolved. AdaFace's selected checkpoint is named MS1MV2, while its separate checkpoints use other corpora; their grants cannot be conflated. AuraFace's publisher does not identify its complete training corpora in the reviewed card. DeepFace wrapper/release metadata does not identify the complete original checkpoint/export/training chain for each graph. These gaps remain explicit blockers; a public model-card license or release digest cannot fill them.

Independent review must obtain exact training-corpus versions/lineage, applicable rights and restrictions, permitted purposes/territories/subjects, withdrawals/removals, original checkpoint/export provenance and overlap with the proposed longitudinal cohort. Do not scrape training data, contact publishers or acquire a private dataset without separate authorization. No new candidate is qualified by this supplementary investigation.
